    About me

    Heyya, I'm Avira Bedi

    My story, as a playbook. Turn the page.

    01The beginning · 2005

    A creative kid in a practical world

    If you're here, someone probably sent you this with the quiet motive of getting me hired. But before the brands and the corporate stuff, a proper introduction.

    I was born on 16 February 2005 in Moradabad, and I've been creatively inclined for as long as I can remember. Give me a school project, a dance or a presentation, anything I could create, and suddenly I cared a lot.

    For a long time, I thought growing up meant setting that aside for something "practical."

    02The ₹10,000 bet · COVID

    Then the world paused, and I started

    Class 10, boards on hold, too much time to think. I found content marketing while scrolling Instagram and asked my parents for ₹10,000 to buy a course. They trusted me with it.

    I learnt content writing, copywriting and social media, then found my first client. By Class 11 I was living two lives: school by day, freelance by night, writing SaaS, lifestyle and beauty blogs for nearly two years.

    03The real question

    From writing words to building brands

    College took me from writing into social media marketing, running calendars and socials for everyone from a celebrity health coach to an EdTech founder.

    Different brands, different audiences, different stories. That's when one question took over: what makes someone care about a brand?

    A few years later, it led me to Hive School.

    04The build · Toemahtoe

    Thirty days, one sock brand, a pitch we won

    At Hive's D2C Sprint, my co-founders and I built Toemahtoe, a quirky D2C socks brand, from product and positioning to content, acquisition and the brand itself.

    We won the investor pitch at Hive's D2C Grand Bazaar.

    05When it clicked

    The same thing, all along

    Somewhere in those thirty days, everything clicked. The girl who loved school projects, the teenager writing blogs after tuition, the freelancer building calendars and the founder building a brand were all chasing the same thing.

    I just took a very long route to realise it was branding.

    06Today

    Still chasing it

    Today, I bring that curiosity, creativity and slightly obsessive love for storytelling into D2C brands as a Brand Manager.

    So yes, this portfolio shows what I can do. I just thought you should first know why I love doing it. And if you're still here, hi again, I'm glad you are.
